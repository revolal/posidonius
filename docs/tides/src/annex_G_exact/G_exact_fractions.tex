\paragraph{Content.} The main document prints the series of $G_{20q}(e)$ and $G_{21q}(e)$ (Tables~C.3 and C.4) with long fractions rounded to 8 significant digits. This annex gives, for \emph{exactly the same terms}, the exact rational coefficients $c_n$ of
\[
  G_{2pq}(e)=\sum_n c_n\,e^n ,
\]
together with their value in double precision.

\paragraph{Origin.} The coefficients are obtained from Tisserand's formula (Kaula 2000, eq.~3.67--3.69), expanded in exact rational arithmetic; no floating-point operation is involved. They are validated against the direct numerical Hansen integral to machine precision.

\paragraph{Symmetries.} $G_{22q}=G_{20,-q}$ and $G_{21q}=G_{21,-q}$; only $G_{20q}$ ($|q|\le24$) and $G_{21q}$ ($q\le0$) are listed. $G_{2pq}=\mathcal O(e^{|q|})$, and only every other power of $e$ appears.

\paragraph{Complete series.} The full series, to $e^{40}$ and for $|q|\le30$, are in
\begin{itemize}
  \item \texttt{data/G\_exact\_l2.txt}: plain text, one line \texttt{l p q n c\_n} per coefficient, exact fractions;
  \item \texttt{data/G\_series.json}: the same, machine-readable (also $l=3$).
\end{itemize}
